# BOZO Vision

## Mission

BOZO exists to make powerful opening training free for every chess player, regardless of rating or budget.

Improving at chess should depend on curiosity, practice, and effort, not on the ability to afford a subscription. If BOZO can reasonably build a useful chess-learning feature and offer it for free, that is what BOZO will strive to do.

## The BOZO Promise

**If we can make it free, it's free.**

Game Review, opening study, recall training, puzzles, coaching, repertoires, and future learning tools are built to help players improve, not to create artificial paywalls.

Voluntary support may help cover hosting, development, analysis, and infrastructure, but contributions should not unlock stronger learning tools or gameplay advantages.

## Product Principles

- Learning comes before monetization.
- BOZO is for every rating level.
- Free should not mean low quality.
- Understanding matters more than memorizing move sequences blindly.
- Training should expose weak positions and help players retain what they learn.
- Review should lead naturally back into practice.
- Features should earn their place by helping players improve.
- Support is optional and should never determine the quality of chess instruction a player receives.

## North Star

**Build the best place to learn chess openings, and keep learning accessible to everyone.**

# BOZO Puzzles v3.6.6 — Arrow Weight

- Arrow shafts increased from 8 to 13 SVG units.
- Arrowheads increased from 18x18 to 24x24 while remaining fixed-size.
- Larger endpoint offset keeps the arrowhead centered cleanly on the target square.
- Squared shafts and sharper knight elbows more closely match conventional chess-board arrows.
- Knight arrows retain the proper orthogonal L route.
- No engine, puzzle-generation, legality, or Supabase behavior changed.

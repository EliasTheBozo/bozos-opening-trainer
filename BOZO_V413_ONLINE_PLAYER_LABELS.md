# BOZO v4.1.3 — Rated Online Player Labels

Rated player-vs-player games no longer present themselves as BOZO Bot games.

Changes:
- RATED ONLINE replaces BOZO BOT ARENA.
- OPPONENT replaces BOZO BOT in the battle strip.
- The opponent's username is shown beside the player's color.
- OPPONENT replaces SELECTED LINE in the sidebar.
- GAME STATUS replaces TRAINING STATUS.
- Online status text is now "Your move." or "Waiting for your opponent to move."
- Online games never mention Stockfish, BOZO Bot, book moves, or opening lessons.
- Bot modes restore the original bot labels when opened.

No Supabase update is required.

WHY BOZO EXISTS

Chess improvement shouldn't depend on your wallet.

BOZO exists to make powerful chess-learning tools available to everyone, regardless of rating or budget. If we can reasonably build a useful training feature and offer it for free, that's what we'll try to do.

Game review. Opening study. Recall training. Puzzles. Coaching. Repertoires. These are learning tools, not privileges.

THE BOZO PROMISE If we can make it free, it's free. Support is always optional and never unlocks stronger chess-learning features.

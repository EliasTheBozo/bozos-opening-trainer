# BOZO Puzzles v3.6.5

- Slimmed user arrows and fixed oversized SVG marker heads.
- Knight annotations render as true L-shaped arrows.
- Puzzle annotation layer aligns to the inside edge of the board border.
- Square highlights snap to square centers with consistent inset.
- Puzzle moves are validated against Chess.js legal moves before engine evaluation.
- Truly illegal moves now receive an explicit legality message.
- Legal but inferior moves are described as legal rather than being conflated with illegality.
- Practical branching window increased from 90cp to 120cp.

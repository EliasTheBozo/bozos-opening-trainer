# BOZO Puzzles v3.6.2

Adds a visible third training mode: **BOZO Puzzles**.

- Positions are generated from legal random play rather than loaded from the opening library.
- Stockfish MultiPV searches for multiple engine-approved continuations.
- Best, strong, and playable moves are accepted instead of enforcing one scripted answer.
- After the player chooses a valid branch, BOZO replies from the actual resulting position with the engine and continues that branch.
- Sessions contain five generated positions and retain the existing score, hint, answer, arrow, and board systems.
- Opening Puzzles remain separate and continue to use published repertoire positions.

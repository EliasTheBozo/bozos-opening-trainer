# BOZO v4.2.0 — Dockable Music Player

Added:
- Drag handle on the floating music player.
- Free dragging while the pointer is held.
- Snap-to-dock on release:
  - bottom-right (default)
  - bottom-left
  - left vertical rail
  - right vertical rail
- Minimize/expand control.
- Dismiss control.
- Navbar Music button restores a dismissed player.
- Player position, collapsed state, and hidden state persist in localStorage.
- Left/right docks switch to a compact vertical presentation.
- Mobile automatically falls back to bottom docking rather than forcing a narrow vertical rail.

No Supabase changes required.

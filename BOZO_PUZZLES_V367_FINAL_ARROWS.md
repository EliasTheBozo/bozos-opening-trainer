# BOZO Puzzles v3.6.7 — Final Arrow Style

- Single-ended arrows only: arrowheads appear only at the destination.
- Knight annotations keep the proper orthogonal L-shape.
- Shaft weight increased from 13 to 17 SVG units.
- Arrowheads increased from 24x24 to 28x28.
- Endpoint shortening increased so the larger head lands cleanly on the target square.
- Slightly lower opacity keeps the heavier arrows readable over pieces and squares.
- No changes to engine, puzzle generation, legality, scoring, or Supabase.
